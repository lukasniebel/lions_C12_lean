Lions' maximal regularity problem: failure at the ½-Hölder endpoint

Lean 4 formalisation of the paper arXiv:2608.11194v2 (Lukas Niebel). Theorem 1.1 (the counterexample on an interval) and Corollary 1.2 (on every bounded domain of every dimension) are proved in Lean in the paper's function spaces, with uniqueness of the Lions solution, from Mathlib alone. The blueprint shows, statement by statement, which Lean declarations formalise the paper and how the formal statements differ from the paper's.
